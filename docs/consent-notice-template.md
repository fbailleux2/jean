# Employee Information Notice — Jean Process Observer

> 📋 **Instructions for HR/Management:** Distribute this notice to all employees
> whose workstations will run the Jean agent, **before** any data collection begins.
> Keep a signed acknowledgement on file. Adapt bracketed fields to your organisation.

---

## What is Jean?

Jean is a tool that observes which business applications you use and the type of
actions you perform (e.g. saving a file, submitting a form), in order to help
**[Organisation]** document how work really happens in practice.

This information is used to improve process documentation and identify gaps between
official procedures and field reality — **not** to monitor individual performance.

---

## What Jean collects

| ✅ Jean captures | ❌ Jean does NOT capture |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Application name (e.g. "SAP", "Excel") | Window title or document name |
| Type of action (save, submit, export…) | What you type or write |
| Time of action (timestamp) | Screen content or screenshots |
| An anonymous session ID | Your name, username, or email |
| A pseudonymous workstation ID | Personal communications (email, chat) |

**Your identity is never directly stored.** Your workstation is represented by a
random identifier (UUID). The link between that identifier and your name is kept
separately by the DPO and is not accessible to the system.

## How long we keep your data

| Data | Retention |
|------|-----------|
| Application transition events | 90 days, then automatically deleted |
| Recurring patterns (anonymised) | 12 months |
| Validated observations | Indefinitely (not linked to individuals) |

## Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

- **Be informed** — this notice fulfils that right
- **Access** your data — contact [DPO email] with your workstation ID
- **Request erasure** — we will delete all events linked to your workstation within 30 days
- **Object to processing** — contact your manager or [DPO email]; your workstation will
  be excluded from Jean within 5 business days
- **Restrict processing** — contact [DPO email]
- **Lodge a complaint** — with the supervisory authority in your country
  (France: CNIL — [www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr))
